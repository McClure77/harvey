Cast

Chris McClure

Chris plays Harvey. He also writes and produces Harvey in Hell.

Romy Evans

Romy Evans plays Katie May, Mrs. Binter and others. Romy is known for The Antiquarium of Sinister Happenings (2023), People Magazine Investigates (2022) and Essence.

Sabrina Knappett

Sabrina Knappett plays Sarah Sue, Anna, Coral and Libra. Sabrina is a Toronto-based actress, writer, and producer whose work spans film, television, and web series, with credits including Soul Heist, Autumn Hill, and Discovery+'s Who the Bleep Did I Marry? She is a six-time Best Actress award winner, known for her versatility on screen and strong character work.

Michael Masurkevitch

Mike plays Clay, Petey and others. He is an actor and screenwriter known for Snow Blind, Pirate Mike, and Good Cop Burha Cop. He also co-produces independent films and recently released his first single, Decompress.

Allen Enlow

Allen plays McCree, Zachary Zane and Verve. Allen is an actor, comedian, and singer — best known for playing Dr. Harry Winer alongside James Gandolfini in The Sopranos (Season 5), and recent appearances in Mr. Robot and The Night Of.

Pete Flatiche

Pete Flatiche plays Sheriff Stillwater, JP Straw and others. He is a veteran performer with a wide range of stage and screen credits, now bringing private investigator extraordinaire JP Straw to life with equal parts menace and humor.
